## New consumer-side gaps (not a demonstrated exploit)

**APP-01 — the new APK selector accepts a syntactically plausible but *failed* source freeze.** In [`scripts/pkforge_emulator_apk_selection.py`](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/blob/e2a72bb6d75f54f31e668de2732966eeb2bd177d/scripts/pkforge_emulator_apk_selection.py), `select_apk()` only requires `frozen_source` to be a dict and `frozen_source.sha256` to match a 64-hex pattern. It does **not** require the producer's three true predicates or a positive file count. For example, an otherwise valid synthetic report with `source_unchanged_after_build: false` can pass this particular consumer check. This does not show that the trusted same-run producer has failed; it shows that the *downstream verifier* does not enforce the producer's complete contract.

**APP-02 — upstream source pins are not rechecked by the selector.** `select_apk()` verifies `pins.mobile_head` but does not compare `pins.pkforge`, `fork_core`, `plugins`, `pkhex_maui_reference`, `official_core_source` or `official_core_package` to the immutable constants already enforced by the attestation producer. A malformed or substituted report with an expected mobile head and matching APK hash could be accepted by this consumer. Keep this as a **P1 provenance-hardening issue** in the trusted ephemeral CI setting, not evidence of a compromised build.

**APP-03 — the source-snapshot digest is only syntax-checked at the consumer boundary.** The producer computes the actual prebuild snapshot digest, but the selector accepts any well-formed 64-hex value without access to the snapshot. Implementation can either pass the same-run snapshot to the selector and compare its SHA-256, or explicitly keep the attestation producer's successful exit as a mandatory same-job prerequisite and test that no externally supplied provenance is accepted. Do not describe a hash-format check as independent source-freeze verification.

It also made active queue claims mandatory and rejects obsolete `claim_branch`/`claim_base_sha` fields. PR #107 released its queue lock. At main `e2a72bb6`, [Foundation](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/actions/runs/40849966825) and [queue](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/actions/runs/40849966713) CI passed. Since then, queue and audit changes do not establish a new compiled APK test.

Draft #83 remains on old head `29c85858ca941e8c92968e0f38c971f4aa34e081`: its shell runner still uses first-match `find` and unsigned fallback, and its legacy queue fields would now be rejected by strict validation. The new selector and recheck are **not wired to an actual `adb install` in the current main workflow**. Rebase #83 and wire both helpers before treating the emulator result as evidence. The existing [ECR-G01–G10](PARITY_EMULATOR_CLAIM_INTEGRATION_GATES_2026-10-29.md) remain the authoritative emulator/claim gates; do not duplicate them.

No APK upload, media rights assertion, original-save export or claim of device certification.

The existing compiled whole-save-share refusal and backup manifest checks remain previously passing at their tested SHAs, not independently re-executed here.
