## Source-specific finding: the last transition is not yet classified

At [PR #118's `scripts/pkforge_emulator_picker_cancel.py`](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/blob/edf71ad0c8d06cd6935bd39b2ca586cae8ee65e6/scripts/pkforge_emulator_picker_cancel.py), `open_picker()` verifies a resumed app, clears main/system logcat, injects `KEYCODE_BUTTON_X=99`, and checks for DocumentsUI. On failure it reports resumed package names and whether the crash buffer has a matching `AndroidRuntime` fatal marker. **No matching Java fatal marker does not prove that the process survived or that no native/.NET crash or Activity finish occurred.** The runner does not capture a bounded pre/post PID, Activity lifecycle transition or app-exit reason around this specific key event. This is a diagnostic-evidence gap, not a demonstrated save defect or proof that X itself caused exit.

The [pinned PKForge HomePage.cs](https://github.com/sofianeelhor/PKForge/blob/2df04430e532b78c247f45b936f3068d8f824987/src/PKForge.App/Views/HomePage.cs) explicitly maps `PadButton.X` and its `X / File` footer to `LinkFileAsync()`, but `OnAppearing()` also dispatches a first-run `DialogueBox.ShowSequenceAsync` followed by `Get started` / `Open a single save file`. Thus a foreground Activity alone cannot prove Home owns the gamepad route when the X event arrives. The [current launch runner](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/blob/edf71ad0c8d06cd6935bd39b2ca586cae8ee65e6/scripts/pkforge_emulator_launch_smoke.sh) also deliberately backgrounds and relaunches the app before invoking the picker. Compare a fresh-launch picker path against the relaunch path; do not silently remove the negative/relaunch regression.
