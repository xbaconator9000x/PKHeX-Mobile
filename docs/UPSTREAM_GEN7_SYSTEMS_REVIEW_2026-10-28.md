## Cross-platform parity and architecture decision

- **Official desktop:** Festival Plaza, facility text/flags, rewards, coins, USUM Battle Agency, Poké Beans and Zygarde cell editing have concrete WinForms routes ([WF L18–40](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/7e26bc53215e4f7bc0041ca375f1042539cb81b3/PKHeX.WinForms/Subforms/Save%20Editors/Gen7/SAV_FestivalPlaza.cs#L18-L40), [WF L276–359](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/7e26bc53215e4f7bc0041ca375f1042539cb81b3/PKHeX.WinForms/Subforms/Save%20Editors/Gen7/SAV_FestivalPlaza.cs#L276-L359), [WB L12–33](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/7e26bc53215e4f7bc0041ca375f1042539cb81b3/PKHeX.WinForms/Subforms/Save%20Editors/Gen7/SAV_Pokebean.cs#L12-L33), [WZ L12–39](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/7e26bc53215e4f7bc0041ca375f1042539cb81b3/PKHeX.WinForms/Subforms/Save%20Editors/Gen7/SAV_ZygardeCell.cs#L12-L39)). These are source entry points, not proof of lossless editing.
- **PKHeX MAUI:** Gen7 trainer, Battle Tree, misc and Ultra tabs exist ([MTab L13–31](https://github.com/santacrab2/PKHeXMAUI/blob/a6f7cfb78e1c96155baa67660fd7ae97895e375f/SaveEditors/TrainerEditors/gen7/Trainertab7.xaml.cs#L13-L31)); they cover trainer and world fields but do not demonstrate the full desktop Festival Plaza facility editor. The Gen7 save route is not transaction-safe on its face. Treat individual pages as **adaptation candidates**, not ready-to-import code.
- **PKForge:** Poké Bean editing and capability-gated world navigation are implemented ([FP L13–60](https://github.com/sofianeelhor/PKForge/blob/2df04430e532b78c247f45b936f3068d8f824987/src/PKForge.App/Views/PokeBeansEditor.cs#L13-L60), [FW L26–81](https://github.com/sofianeelhor/PKForge/blob/2df04430e532b78c247f45b936f3068d8f824987/src/PKForge.App/Views/WorldEventsMenu.cs#L26-L81)). The examined World & events menu does not route Festival Plaza/Zygarde; this is a menu-level observation, **not** a repo-wide absence claim.
- **Our Android project:** official pinned-Core immutable inspection, synthetic SAF fault checks and isolated candidate/backup safety primitives are useful, but independent Gen7 desktop-matched fixtures, physical device tests, authenticated layouts and any save-publication path remain absent. Maintain read-only behavior for all unverified and hack-ambiguous inputs.
- **Recommended architecture:** keep official Core as the canonical format engine; adapt tested PKForge navigation/transaction UX and selected MAUI editors behind document-scoped drafts and strict profile gates. Do **not** copy forked serializers, live `MainPage.sav` bindings or bundled art without independent review.

## Prioritized implementer tickets (documentation only)

1. **P0 Gen7 layout boundary:** block Festa facility index 7, invalid message/prize indices, and raw block aliases; assert currency and neighbor sentinels untouched (Y01–Y03, parity YG01–YG06).
2. **P0 transactional UI:** eliminate static save/page references and all live mutation on field changes or Save-tab navigation; cancel, invalid input, document switch and exception must preserve original bytes (Y23–Y26, Y28, Y31; YG23–YG32).
3. **P0 destructive normalizations:** reproduce Hall of Fame alias, Battle Agency invited-bit zeroing, desktop bean negative wrap and Battle Tree oversized setter partial writes using synthetic data only (Y10, Y13, Y18, Y21).
4. **P1 semantic parity:** map 93 resort slots, 15 bean counters, 95/100 Zygarde cells, 50 Battle Tree trainers, four battle types, USUM-only Agency, and camera version 1–5 (Y08–Y17, Y27–Y30).
5. **Release gate:** independently baseline same-version desktop PKHeX 26.10.18 output and raw checksums for each claimed game; separately validate Android provider recovery, backup durability and byte-scoped Save As. Never authorize unknown hacks based on retail-looking headers.
