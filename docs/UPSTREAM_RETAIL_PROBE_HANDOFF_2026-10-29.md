## P0 audit finding: negative tests need a discriminating control

The [18-assertion smoke](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/blob/c8b3d76c7192aa32fd56e188056cb15129916c5b/tests/PKHeXMobile.RetailProbe.Smoke/Program.cs) creates zero-filled 0x20000 buffers with eight footer stamps, a zero-filled Luminescent-sized buffer with one header, and 37 arbitrary bytes. Those can be rejected by the underlying parser **even if the retail-only guard is absent**. Thus the passing smoke proves these particular invalid inputs were refused, but does not by itself prove the guard blocks an otherwise valid-looking retail save carrying a hack signature. The script's [RejectKnownHackLayout](https://github.com/xbaconator9000x/PKHeX-Mobile/blob/c8b3d76c7192aa32fd56e188056cb15129916c5b/scripts/pkforge_retail_only_probe.py) is source-inspected, not yet independently mutation-tested. This is a **coverage gap, not evidence of a bypass**.

Fast discriminator: generate a *valid, synthetic* retail fixture through pinned official Core, confirm the baseline parser accepts it, alter only controlled signature/layout fields to trigger each guard, then prove a deliberately guard-disabled diagnostic accepts or reaches the unsafe retail parse while the guarded version refuses. If no safe mutated fixture can be made valid, explicitly mark the guard-effect claim **unproven** and require a consented private fixture. Test eight versus seven sector stamps, full/trimmed SRAM, RetroArch container, Radical Red structural detector and Compass key positive/negative controls; no private saves in Git.
