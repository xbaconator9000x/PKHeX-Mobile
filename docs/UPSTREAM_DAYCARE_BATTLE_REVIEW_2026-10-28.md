## Architecture, licences and testability

Use PKHeX.Core as the authoritative parser, but never expose its mutable save buffers or call any setters during inspection. A read-only Android view must operate on detached immutable snapshots with original-byte SHA-256 and authenticated format identity. For future edits, use a clone, validate target protection and allowlisted byte deltas, preserve a durable backup, re-open with the exact pinned Core and independently compare with same-version desktop PKHeX before any provider publication. No unsupported ROM-hack may be serialized as vanilla.

The pinned Core checkpoint identifies GPL-3.0-or-later; PKForge carries a GPLv3 licence file, while MAUI's exact code/asset licensing requires a separate provenance review. This audit imports **no code, binaries or artwork**. Preserve attribution and GPL source/distribution obligations, verify every borrowed asset independently, and never commit ROMs, saves, proprietary game art, secrets or conversation content.

**Evidence limits:** Findings are source-traced hazards or semantics, not Android runtime reproductions. Forge's single USUM synthetic test ([F:ft L9-42](https://github.com/sofianeelhor/PKForge/blob/2df04430e532b78c247f45b936f3068d8f824987/tests/PKForge.Engine.Tests/DaycareTests.cs#L9-L42)) is not a generation-wide certificate. All 39 companion scenarios remain unexecuted; CI compilation alone is insufficient.
