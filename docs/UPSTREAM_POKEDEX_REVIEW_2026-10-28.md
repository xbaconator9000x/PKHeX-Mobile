## Architecture disposition

1. Keep official pinned PKHeX.Core as the canonical format engine behind immutable, revision-aware read-only projections. Neither MAUI's off-by-one dex editor nor Forge's unsupported-hack no-op behavior is safe to copy wholesale.
2. Distinguish species identity, regional dex index, DLC block revision, obtained/seen/displayed/form/language state, and write authorization. A recognized envelope or legal Pokémon never certifies an unknown ROM-hack layout.
3. Future editing must use an isolated draft and compare the complete entry and enclosing block. Bulk completion, registration, living-dex generation and single-bit changes have different side effects and permissions.
4. Keep unknown and unsupported hack dexes read-only. Require authenticated versioned profiles, negative fixtures, independent desktop parity and transactional backup/readback before any export.
5. Official Core is GPL-3.0-or-later ([CL L9-18](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/7e26bc53215e4f7bc0041ca375f1042539cb81b3/PKHeX.Core/PKHeX.Core.csproj#L9-L18)); separately clear third-party graphics, fonts and binaries. This pass imported none.

**Milestone 0 remains open:** no reproducible pinned candidate builds, independent same-version desktop save corpus, Android provider/device evidence or full asset-rights inventory was produced by this source audit. The companion gate matrix is a proposed test plan, not certification.
