## Architecture, format and licensing disposition

**Official Core:** Keep the exact pinned PKHeX.Core as read-only engine. A future immutable move snapshot must preserve four raw move IDs, current PP, PP Ups, relearn slots, TR record indices, mapped move IDs, permits and raw flags separately. Derived max PP and legality must be explicitly contextual. SetMoves, FixMoves, healing and TR bulk applicators are mutating; they belong only on detached drafts with full-field diffs and rollback. The ZA desktop TM index discrepancy is a reproducible source-level hypothesis, not a patched or runtime-confirmed bug.

**Android/UI:** Do not adopt MAUI's global entity, static move list, PP text binding or TR handlers.
