## Implementation architecture and testability

1. **Projection before editing.** Add an immutable, versioned `PokemonTrainingProjection` with stored IV/EV six-tuples in Core order, format-specific EV cap, raw nature and stat nature, hyper flags, AV/GV presence and values, Tera original/override/effective, plus provenance and source hash. Unknown interfaces are **unsupported**, not zero-filled.
2. **No Core mutation in presentation.** Project from an isolated clone/snapshot; do not call `ResetPartyStats`, `FixMoves`, `SetRandomIVs`, `HPType` setters or flag setters in read-only code. A before/after SHA-256 comparison must prove reads are byte-preserving.
